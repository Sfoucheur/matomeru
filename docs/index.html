Matomeru

A Windows desktop app for managing a Magic: The Gathering collection in bulk.

What it does

  • Holds a collection of cards with finish, condition, language and quantity, and prices it from Scryfall.
  • Imports and exports CSV, and adds cards in bulk by name or set number.
  • Syncs decks from Archidekt and shows which cards a deck is missing.
  • Moves cards between decks and the collection, and tracks what has left a deck before the decklist catches up.
  • Builds pull lists for cards you are taking out, sold or traded.
  • Works out booster odds from set data.
  • Breaks the collection down by set, rarity and language, with its total value.

Where your data lives

On your machine, in %APPDATA%\matomeru — one SQLite database and a cache of card images. There is no account to create, no server of ours to talk to, and nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you ask for it.

Backing up to Google Drive

Optional, and off until you turn it on. Pressing Ctrl+S offers to copy a snapshot of the database to a folder in your own Google Drive, and the same dialog can read it back to restore.

The app asks Google for a single narrow permission — drive.file — which lets it see only the files it created itself or the folder you explicitly chose through Google's own folder picker. The rest of your Drive stays invisible to it, and that is enforced by Google rather than promised by us. You can withdraw the permission at any time from your Google account.

Privacy

No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, and no data sent to the developer. The full detail, including every service the app contacts, is in the privacy policy.
