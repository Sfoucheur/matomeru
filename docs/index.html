Matomeru

A Windows app for handling a Magic: The Gathering collection in bulk — for when a box of cards lands on the table and needs to become a list.

Download

Installerrecommended Matomeru-Setup-x.y.z.exe — installs normally, adds a shortcut, and can update itself when a new version appears. Portable Matomeru x.y.z.exe — runs from wherever you put it, installs nothing. It tells you when an update exists but cannot replace itself.

Both links go to the releases page, where the files are attached to the newest release. If that page is empty, there has not been a release yet — the app is built from source in the meantime.

Windows will warn you about an unrecognised app. That warning is accurate: the build is not code-signed, which means nobody has paid a certificate authority to vouch for it. Choose More info then Run anyway if you are happy to. See Troubleshooting.

Getting started

There is nothing to set up. Open the app and it is ready: no account, no sign-in, no server. Everything lives on your machine from the first launch.

Adding your first cards

  • By name — the Add cards screen shows every printing of a card in every language, so you can pick the exact one you are holding.
  • By set and collector number — faster when you are working through a sorted pile.
  • From a CSV — Import / export reads files from ManaBox, Moxfield and Deckbox, or anything you can map to columns yourself.

Connecting your decks

Put your Archidekt username in Settings and the Decks screen syncs your decklists. The app then answers "where is this card?" — which deck a card is already sleeved into, and which cards a deck is still missing.

What it does

The collection

A table or a gallery of card images, whichever suits what you are doing. Localised names lead, with the English name alongside when they differ. Filter by language, rarity, set, finish, condition, colour, type or price; sort on two levels at once; resize the grid with Ctrl+scroll.

Card details

Click any card anywhere for one surface with the large image, the set and rarity, current prices, every other printing in every language, which of your decks it sits in, and the odds of pulling that exact printing from each booster the set comes in.

Decks

Synced from Archidekt. Deck label colours get a meaning you choose, so the app knows which sleeved cards count as yours. Cards move between a deck and the collection in both directions, and the app keeps track of what has left a deck before Archidekt has caught up.

Pull lists

For cards on their way out — sold, traded, or moving into a deck. An open list reserves rather than removes: nothing disappears from your collection until you confirm it has actually gone, and a confirmed list can be reverted if you change your mind.

Prices and totals

Cached Scryfall prices in USD or EUR, with the collection broken down by set, rarity and language.

Language and appearance

English or French, following your Windows language by default, and twelve colour themes in light or dark.

Keyboard shortcuts

KeysWhat happens
Ctrl+S Back up to Google Drive. Asks first — the keystroke never sends anything by itself.
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y Undo and redo, covering every local edit you make.
Alt+1 … Alt+7 Jump straight to a screen.
Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- / Ctrl+0 Bigger cards, smaller cards, reset.
Ctrl+scroll The same, over any grid of cards.

Inside a text box these are left alone — Ctrl+Z there undoes your typing, as it should.

Backing up to Google Drive

Optional, and off until you turn it on. Settings → Backup → Connect with Google Drive: your browser opens Google's own consent page, you approve, and that is the whole setup. Your Google password is typed on Google's site and never reaches this app.

After that, Ctrl+S offers to send a copy of your collection to a folder in your Drive, and the same dialog reads it back to restore. You choose the folder's name; the app creates it at the top of your Drive if it is not there already.

  • The app can only see files it created. Google enforces that — the permission requested does not allow it to look at anything else in your Drive.
  • The backup is your database, compressed — roughly 4 MB for a 30 MB collection. Card images are left out; they re-download on their own.
  • Restoring replaces everything on this machine, and keeps a copy of what was there first in case that was not what you wanted.
  • You can withdraw access whenever you like, from Settings or from your Google account.

Updates

The installed version looks for a new release shortly after it starts and says nothing unless there is one. When there is, Settings → Updates offers to download it and install on restart. You can check whenever you like, or turn the startup check off entirely.

The portable version cannot replace itself, so it opens the release page instead and leaves the download to you.

Where your data lives

In %APPDATA%\matomeru — one database file and a cache of card images, deliberately outside the app folder so reinstalling or updating never touches them. Deleting that folder deletes everything the app holds.

The app has no server. It contacts Scryfall for card data and images, MTGJSON for booster contents, Archidekt for your decklists if you give it a username, and Google only if you turn on backup. Nothing is sent to the developer, ever. The privacy policy lists all of it.

Troubleshooting

"Windows protected your PC"

SmartScreen shows this for applications it has not seen signed by a known publisher. Matomeru is not code-signed, so it will appear. Choose More info then Run anyway. If you would rather not, building from source produces the same app.

The portable version never updates itself

Correct, and it cannot: there is no installer for it to hand over to. It notices new releases and opens the release page so you can download the new one. Use the installer if you want updates handled for you.

Settings says the build has no Google client

That build was compiled without Drive credentials, so backup cannot work in it. Official releases include them; a build made from source needs its own — see the developer notes in the repository.

A deck will not sync

Archidekt has to be able to see it. Private decks are not readable by anything but you signed in on their site, so the app reports them as unavailable rather than guessing.
