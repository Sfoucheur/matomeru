Privacy policy

Matomeru · last updated 23 August 2026

Matomeru is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no user accounts, collects no analytics, and sends nothing to its developer. There is no server operated by this application.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app knows lives in a single folder on your computer — on Windows, %APPDATA%\matomeru. It holds one SQLite database (your cards, decks, pull lists and settings) and a cache of card images downloaded for display.

Nothing in that folder is transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly ask the app to back it up (see below). Deleting the folder deletes everything the app holds.

Services the app contacts

The app makes requests to the following services, and only for the purposes listed. None of these requests carry your name, your email address, or any identifier the app has created for you.

ServiceWhyWhen
Scryfall Card data, prices and card images When you search, add cards, or refresh prices
MTGJSON Set and booster-pack composition data When you load booster data for a set
Archidekt Reading your public decklists Only if you enter an Archidekt username or deck link
Google Backing up to and restoring from your Drive Only if you connect Google Drive, and only when you ask for a backup

Google Drive backup

This feature is optional and inactive until you connect it. When you do, the app opens Google's own consent page in your browser; your Google password is entered on Google's website and is never seen by this application.

The app requests exactly one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. Google restricts that permission to files the application itself created, plus any folder you explicitly select through Google's own folder picker. The application cannot list, read or modify anything else in your Drive.

  • What is uploaded: a snapshot of the local database described above — your cards, decks, pull lists and app settings — as a single file, into a folder of your choosing. Card images are not uploaded.
  • Who can read it: you. The file lives in your Drive, under your account. The developer has no access to it and no way to obtain it.
  • Access tokens: stored only on your computer, encrypted using the operating system's own credential protection (DPAPI on Windows). They are never transmitted anywhere except back to Google when refreshing access.
  • What the app learns about your account: nothing. It does not request your name, email address or profile, and never receives them.
  • Withdrawing access: at any time, either with the Disconnect button in the app's settings, or from your Google account permissions page. Revoking access leaves any backups already in your Drive intact, and you may delete them yourself like any other file.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no data shared with or sold to third parties.
  • No account registration, and no personal details requested by the app.
